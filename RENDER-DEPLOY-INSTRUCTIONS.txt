Required Render Environment Variables:
1. GEMINI_API_KEY = your Google AI Studio key
2. SMTP_HOST = SMTP server hostname from your email provider
3. SMTP_PORT = usually 465 or 587, as provided by your email provider
4. SMTP_USER = SMTP account username/email
5. SMTP_PASS = SMTP password or app password supplied by your email provider
6. ENQUIRY_TO_EMAIL = inbox that should receive enquiries

Email delivery will NOT work until valid SMTP values are configured. Do not commit secrets to GitHub.
For Gmail SMTP, use smtp.gmail.com, port 465 (SSL) or 587 (STARTTLS), and an app password if your Google account supports it. Do not use your regular account password.
